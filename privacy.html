Who is responsible

Ladata Portugal, Lda, NIF 518236870, Rua Tomaz Ribeiro 550, 4450-298 Matosinhos, Portugal, is responsible for your data. For any privacy question, email info@bookladata.com.

02

What we collect and why

  • Orders: your name, email, phone, delivery and billing address and, if you give it, your tax number. We use them to deliver your order, send you emails about it, invoice it and handle returns or warranty claims. Legal basis: performing our contract with you and our legal (tax) obligations.
  • Newsletter: your email, only if you sign up. We use it to send you news and offers. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link.
  • Messages: what you send us by email or WhatsApp, to answer you.
03

Who processes data for us

  • Stripe, for payments. Your card details go straight to Stripe, never to us.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website and stores newsletter sign-ups.
  • Resend, which sends our order emails.
  • FedEx, which receives your name, address and phone to deliver your parcel.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the website's typeface.

Some of these companies may process data outside the European Union. When they do, they use the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

04

How long we keep it

Order and invoicing data are kept for 10 years, as required by Portuguese tax law. Newsletter data are kept until you unsubscribe.

05

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website stores your shopping bag and shop filters in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there when you come back. When you pay, Stripe may use its own cookies to keep the payment secure.

06

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, to limit how we use it, or object to its use, by emailing info@bookladata.com.

If you think we have not handled your data correctly, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD.

Questions about your data?

Write to us at info@bookladata.com or send us a WhatsApp message.
